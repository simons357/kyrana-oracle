Privacy Policy

Living Words Gematria · Effective November 21, 2026 · Published by Jonathan Simons

Living Words Gematria is a study app for letter values. It is designed to work on the user's device. This policy matches an App Store privacy label of Data Not Collected.

Data collection

Living Words Gematria does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or SDKs that collect data. Everything someone types stays on this device. The app does not send it to a server.

Data stored on the device

Notes, custom letter maps, a typed name, an optional birthdate, and appearance settings are stored only on that device, using the app's local storage. They never leave the device unless the user chooses to export or share them through the system share sheet or a local file, in which case the user decides where they go.

Optional reference links

Four optional reference links in Sources & Method can open in the system browser if the user follows them. The app does not send the user's name, notes, or birthdate along with that tap.

Deleting data

Notes can be cleared inside the app (Notebook → Clear). Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
