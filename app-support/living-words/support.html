Living Words Gematria Support

Living Words is a study app for the traditional craft of treating letters as numbers — Hebrew gematria, Greek isopsephy, Arabic abjad, and later English counting methods — for reflection and for fun. It is an educational tool. A shared total is an observation. It does not, by itself, prove a meaning, a cause, or a destiny, and it does not predict a life.

Common questions

Where are my notes? On this device only. Use Notebook → Back up all data to save a copy, and Restore backup to bring it back. Deleting the app removes what is stored on the device.

Why does my total differ from another source? Totals depend on spelling, final letter forms, diacritics, and the chosen system. Check the system name shown with each result.

Does the app need internet? No. The app works without a network connection. Four optional reference links in Sources & Method can open in the system browser if you follow them.

Privacy: see the Privacy Policy. The app does not collect data. Notes, a saved name, custom maps, and your appearance stay on this device.
